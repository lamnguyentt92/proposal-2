Reviewer note: points 2, 3 and 4 share one root. The specification replaced the per-template correlation with the right test (point 6) but has not yet written down the numbers and sampling rules that make that test able to fail in a prespecified way. §13.1 says the same ("Must be shown by pilot (b)"), so "answered in design" there should be read as "test chosen", not "objection closed".
