# Cross-model adversarial review — P1

**How to use (manual route):** open a new chat with a model from another family (e.g. GPT or Gemini), attach the files listed below in full, paste everything under the line, and save the complete answer verbatim as `XMODEL_REDTEAM_P1_RESPONSE.md` with the model name and date.

**Files to attach:** `PROPOSAL.md` (= 09_IDEA_SPEC_P1_ACL.md); `ideation/01_VENUES.md`; from `ideation/05_NOVELTY/`: IDEA_L1_priorart_A.md, IDEA_L1_priorart_B.md, IDEA_L1S_drift_scan.md.

---

Role: you are a demanding referee for ACL 2027 (via ACL Rolling Review), area Discourse and Pragmatics, reviewing a research idea before any work has been done. You have no stake in it.

Task:
1. Scores 1–5 with one-line reasons: novelty, significance, technical depth, soundness potential, evaluation, clarity, venue fit.
2. Likely reasons for rejection, most serious first.
3. The strongest baseline a referee will demand that the specification does not already include.
4. The single experiment most likely to come out negative, and what that would mean.
5. Boundary cases or counterexamples for each claim in section 5: the condition under which the claim fails or becomes vacuous.
6. A kill argument: the single strongest case for rejecting the eventual paper, about 200 words, one committed argument.
7. What would make this a clear accept.

Rules: judge technical, ethical and resource feasibility only; do not judge team size, workload or deadlines. If you rely on a published paper, give its identifier and say whether you opened it.
