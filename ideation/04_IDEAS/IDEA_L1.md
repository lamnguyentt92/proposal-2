# IDEA_L1: Caution Is Not Composition — memory writing as unframed speech report, tested with cue-matched scope twins (EN) and frame-flip pairs (VI)

- Gap addressed: G1 (primary); touches G12 only through the translate-then-extract baseline.

- Target venue and track: ACL 2027 via ARR — "Discourse and Pragmatics" (first choice) or "Semantics: Lexical and Sentence-Level"; secondary area "Dialogue and Interactive Systems". ARR contribution types: data resource + model analysis.

- Problem (1 paragraph): A memory writer turns a dialogue turn into a context-free record ("User is moving to Hanoi"). Prior work shows that speculative plans get stored as facts (AgentMemGate), that 17–21% of natural writes are unsupported (DerivAudit), and that third-party content gets filed under the user (MemAlign taxonomy, 15 cases). The same prior work also suggests the simple case is nearly solved by prompting: AgentMemGate's "cautious extractor" cut contamination to 2.5% on its core set. What nobody has measured is whether that fix is a real semantic capability or a cue-triggered refusal to write. The two come apart exactly where operators nest (report over conditional over negation over quotation) and where the language does not mark person and quotation the way English does. If writers are merely cautious, they will (a) still promote wrong content when the cue is absent or non-canonical and (b) silently drop content the user did commit to whenever a cue word is present. Neither failure is visible in any existing memory benchmark, because none contains items whose cue inventory is held constant while the correct write decision flips.

- Key insight (the non-obvious observation; 2–3 sentences): A memory write is an indirect speech report whose reporting frame has been deleted ("the user said that p" → "p, about the user"), and deleting the frame is licensed only if every operator on the path from the utterance root to p is commitment-transparent *for the user* — a property of scope order and operator class (factive vs non-factive vs counter-factive attitude, direct vs indirect quote, self vs other source), not of which cue words occur. Hence one can build twins that share the same bag of cues but have opposite gold write decisions ("Lan doesn't know I quit" licenses the write; "Lan doesn't think I quit" does not), on which any cue-based "cautious" policy must trade blocked promotions one-for-one against lost licensed facts. Vietnamese sharpens this into something English cannot host: because the person value of a kin term (chị, anh, em, con) is not lexically fixed but licensed by a speaker–addressee dyad, and because Vietnamese has no tense backshift, no obligatory quotation marking and freely null embedded subjects, a *string-identical* reported sentence receives different gold holders under a different previously established xưng–hô frame — a minimal pair with zero lexical difference in the target turn.

- Approach (how the insight becomes a method/system/theory):
  0. Relation to SCOPE-INTERVENTIONS v5. Kept: nested operator composition with held-out combinations, quotation / reported-speech attribution at write time, Vietnamese person reference (kin terms as person markers, null subjects), human-authored data with human gold frames. Cut: the authenticated permission ledger (RQ2) and single-operator hedging as a headline; "stage-wise accounting" and the three-arm estimand are no longer claimed as novel and survive only as a secondary read-out.
  1. Frame calculus (small, explicit). Each scored proposition gets a gold tuple ⟨holder, polarity, condition, source chain, anchor evidence span, user-commitment ∈ {committed-p, committed-not-p, attributed-only, conditional-only, none, genuinely ambiguous}⟩. The write decision is derived from the tuple by a fixed rule table, which settles the negation issue the audit raised: "stored not-p as a user fact" is scored separately from "failed to store" and from "stored p".
  2. Layer A — scope twins, English and Vietnamese, natively authored in each language (not translated). Operator inventory: REPORT-other (indirect), QUOTE-other (direct), REPORT/QUOTE-self, COND (antecedent / consequent), NEG, ATT (factive / non-factive / counter-factive), TEMPORAL-limit. A family = one asserted base turn + its single-operator variants + at least one *license twin* and one *block twin* with matched cue inventory, at depth 2 and 3. Example twins: 'Lan said, "I'm allergic to peanuts"' vs 'I said to Lan, "I'm allergic to peanuts"'; "If Lan says I should quit, I will" vs "Lan says that if I'm unhappy I should quit".
  3. Layer B — Vietnamese-only perspective items (no English counterpart by construction):
     - Frame-flip pairs. Target turn held constant, e.g. "Lan bảo chị nên nghỉ việc để đi học tiếp."; in context A the user self-refers as *em* and Lan is her senior (chị = Lan → Lan thinks she herself should quit); in context B the user self-refers as *chị* and Lan is her junior (chị = user → Lan advised the user). [Readings to be confirmed by native annotators; ambiguous instances go to the "genuinely ambiguous" class.]
     - Null subject under *bảo*. "Mẹ bảo về quê ăn Tết." is either directive ("mother told me to come home", ∅ = user) or assertive ("mother said she is going", ∅ = mother); in neither reading is "User will travel home for Tết" a user commitment. [Needs native-linguist confirmation of the directive/assertive split of *bảo*.]
     - Attitude triple *biết / nghĩ / tưởng*: "Lan biết / nghĩ / tưởng em nghỉ việc rồi" → committed-p / none / suggests not-p. [The counter-factive force of *tưởng* is a strong default, possibly cancellable — confirm.]
     - Non-canonical negation without *không*: "Em có nghỉ việc đâu", "Em đâu có nghỉ việc" (= I did not quit). [Confirm inventory and regional spread.]
  4. Held-out composition split. Few-shot exemplars and any tuned extractor see all single operators and a fixed subset of pairs; test items use unseen pairs and all triples. Whole families are held out.
  5. Secondary method probe, "lazy disquotation": keep the reporting frame in the record (source chain + operator stack + verbatim anchor) and decide at read time whether p may be used as a user fact. Measured against eager writing on the same items to see whether errors merely move from write to read.

- Contribution type: new measurement/benchmark (primary); small new method (lazy disquotation) as a secondary, clearly labelled probe.

- Claims (falsifiable, 2–4):
  - C1 (twin dissociation). For prompt-level variants of a given backbone, block-twin accuracy and license-twin accuracy are negatively related (a trade-off frontier), while on single-operator items both can be high at once; paired twin accuracy of the cautious extractor falls at least 20 points below its single-operator accuracy. Refuted if a cautious or structured extractor reaches paired accuracy within 5 points of its single-operator accuracy.
  - C2 (composition, not coverage). Accuracy on held-out operator combinations is lower than on seen combinations of the same depth by a margin that exceeds the family-clustered 95% interval, for at least the memory frameworks' native writers; depth alone (seen depth-3 vs seen depth-2) explains less than combination novelty.
  - C3 (dyad anchoring). On Vietnamese frame-flip pairs, the rate at which a system assigns the *same* holder to both members of a pair (which is always wrong for one of them) exceeds 50% for native framework writers and remains above the human disagreement rate for the best structured extractor; translate-to-English-then-extract does not close the gap, because translation forces an early choice of "I / she / you".
  - C4 (optional, method). Lazy disquotation reduces false unconditional promotion to near zero by construction but its total later-answer error is lower than eager writing only if read-time use of framed records is accurate; the claim is that total error drops on depth ≥ 2 items. Could equally come out as "errors only relocate".

- Closest work known so far (with identifiers) and the expected delta:
  - AgentMemGate, arXiv:2610.07707 — one operator family (speculative plans), five-level hedge sweep, LLM-generated English, cautious extractor at 2.5%. Delta: cue-matched twins make the cautious extractor's cost measurable; nesting; quotation; human-authored; Vietnamese.
  - DerivAudit, arXiv:2609.36130 — free-form, model-labelled "semantic obligations" on LoCoMo/HaluMem writes; correct/distorted/absent arms. Delta: operator-typed human gold frames; controlled composition; the three-arm design is reused, not claimed.
  - HaluMem, arXiv:2511.03506 — stage-wise scores; distractors are assistant-asserted facts. Delta: user utterances under reportative, conditional, negative, quotative scope.
  - MemAlign / ConMem, arXiv:2610.33432, with the taxonomy arXiv:2609.33623 — attribution_confusion exists as a coarse category with 15 cases, synthetic contexts. Delta: attribution decomposed by report type and anchoring, hundreds of human-written cases.
  - TimlMemBench, ACL Anthology 2026.findings-emnlp.507 — governed-memory families in Chinese/English, synthetic then human-corrected; SKIP covers unsupported candidates. Delta: no operator-level labels or contrasts there; Vietnamese absent.
  - PASB, arXiv:2607.10526 — status promotion 51.4%, attribution removal 33.1% for sycophancy-inducing content with four coarse framings. Delta: truth-conditionally non-asserted content with compositional structure; our "attribution removal" is predicted per operator stack.
  - AuthMem-Bench ("When Memory Becomes Authority"), arXiv:2608.01679 — consolidation keeps a claim but drops source constraints, in 48 of 49 configurations [abstract-level only; found in this session]. Delta: it types the *authority class* of the source; we type the *linguistic scope path*; must be cited and contrasted.
  - Gated Memory, arXiv:2610.11270; The Memory Trap, arXiv:2611.07554; MemTrace, arXiv:2606.17328; MemGuard, arXiv:2605.28009 — baselines / positioning for write-time vs read-time.
  - Linguistic neighbours: Chinese zero pronouns, arXiv:2605.31056 (read in this session: LLMs translate fewer than half of zero pronouns, and its error table shows a zero subject after 他说 "he said" resolved to the wrong participant) — direct prior evidence that null subjects under report are hard; SEAM, arXiv:2610.04210 (unmarked seams between pasted text and user speech; abstract-level) — the quotation-without-marks problem outside memory; event-factuality line arXiv:1804.02472, 2407.15352, 2502.08777, 2107.00807 (abstract-level in the audit); TWIST, arXiv:2609.28575 (agreement warning: κ 0.565 raw).
  - Vietnamese person reference: Luong (1990), *Discursive Practices and Linguistic Meanings: The Vietnamese System of Person Reference*, John Benjamins, Pragmatics & Beyond NS 11 (publisher record fetched in this session); a corpus chapter on null vs overt pronouns in Vietnamese narratives in Duffield, Phan & Trinh (eds.) 2019, Studies in Language Companion Series 211, pp. 275–300 (publisher page fetched; chapter authors/title not shown on the page — verify before citing). Classical background on plugs/holes/filters (Karttunen 1973) and indexical shift (Schlenker 2003) was seen only as search listings — verify bibliographic details before citing.

- Evaluation sketch: settings, baselines (strongest first), metrics, decisive experiment
  - Size (against the audit's calibration of 1,000–5,000 scored items, 8–30 configurations, reported agreement): Layer A ≈ 300 families per language × 5–6 variants ≈ 1,600 turns per language, about 2–2.5k scored propositions per language; Layer B ≈ 250 families × 2–3 variants ≈ 600 turns. Total ≈ 4,500–5,500 scored propositions. Every item double-annotated by two native annotators, third-party adjudication blind to model output; κ reported per layer (holder, polarity, condition, commitment); the ambiguous class is kept and scored with admissible-set matching rather than deleted.
  - Systems (target 15–20 configurations): native writers of three memory frameworks (e.g. Mem0, Graphiti/Zep, Letta or A-MEM) × 2–3 backbones (commercial + one open model on local GPU), plus extractor-only conditions.
  - Baselines, strongest first: (1) strong structured extractor on a frontier reasoning model that must output source chain, operator stack, anchor and commitment before deciding, with few-shot exemplars restricted to seen combinations; (2) AgentMemGate's cautious extractor, re-implemented from the paper; (3) Gated Memory, labelled approximation (no code release was seen); (4) for Vietnamese, translate-to-English-then-(1); (5) full-dialogue-in-context reader with no memory (comprehension reference, not a competitor); (6) naive extract–store.
  - Metrics: paired twin accuracy (both members right); false unconditional promotion and licensed-fact retention (presented as adaptations of AgentMemGate's contamination / over-holding and MemAlign's store / do-not-store recall); same-holder rate on frame-flip pairs; seen-vs-held-out combination gap; family-clustered bootstrap intervals. Secondary: native-vs-oracle record contrast on later answers, broken down by operator stack; human-judged subset for any LLM-judged endpoint.
  - Decisive experiment: baseline (1) on held-out-combination twins in both languages and on Vietnamese frame-flip pairs. If its paired accuracy is within 5 points of its single-operator accuracy and its same-holder rate is at the human-disagreement floor, the key insight is empirically dead for frontier extractors and the paper reduces to a negative result plus framework-level findings.

- Resources needed vs available (00_CONSTRAINTS.md): commercial APIs for writers, extractors and judges — available; one open model (roughly 8–30B) served on 1–2 GPUs — available; ≥2 native Vietnamese annotators fluent in English for authoring/annotating Vietnamese and for English annotation — available (English items by fluent non-native annotators are acceptable for labelling; natural English *authoring* would benefit from one native or near-native checker — to confirm). No training at scale. No user data: all dialogues fictional.

- Risks and unknowns:
  - Frontier models may solve English twins when asked to reason; then English Layer A is a negative result and the weight shifts to Layer B and to framework-native writers.
  - Agreement: TWIST saw κ 0.565 on comparable commitment contrasts; Vietnamese perspective items contain real ambiguity. Mitigation is the admissible-set scoring and reporting agreement per layer, not filtering to a clean subset.
  - Several Vietnamese judgments above (the *bảo* split, *tưởng*, non-canonical negation, the frame-flip readings) are my reading and must be confirmed by a native linguist; regional variation (North/South) may change kin-term defaults.
  - Scoop risk is the highest of the three cards: four neighbouring papers appeared in the eight weeks before the audit.
  - Naturalness: twins engineered for cue matching can read as artificial; needs a naturalness rating per item.
