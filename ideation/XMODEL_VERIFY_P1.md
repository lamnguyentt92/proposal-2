## Factual errors and imprecisions noticed in the reviews

No fabricated or mis-identified citation was found. All identifiers, titles, venues and dates given in the two reviews for the 12 priority items match the primary records. The following are the only points where a description is looser than the source:

1. Item 4 (Nguyen and Sim): "reports person-sensitive distributional effects" overstates the result. Person has no significant effect within a generation; the one effect is a Gen2 shift from kin terms to pronouns for second-person objects. The study is about object omission in heritage Vietnamese in Canberra, not about person reference in general.
2. Item 3 (Korean study): "builds Korean-language multi-session conversation data" is correct only in the sense of extending an existing Korean set (KMSC to KEEM). The paper is mainly a fine-tuning study.
3. Item 10 (Manufactured Confidence): the red-team review presents the verbatim control as precedent for a "budget-matched, context-preserving episodic memory" baseline. The control exists, but it is a small poisoning-test control, not budget-matched and not a retrieval-QA baseline.
4. Item 9 (DIAL-SUMMER): both reviews are correct, but anyone reusing the citation should know that VIEWPOINT DISTORTION means "reported content stated as fact", not "attributed to the wrong person"; only SPEAKER MISATTRIBUTION is a wrong-person category.
5. Item 5 (Tạp chí Ngân hàng): correct, but the source is a practitioner journal article with no numeric result for the address-form criterion; "reports that they do not adapt satisfactorily" rests on one sentence and one screenshot.
6. Item 1 (CogMem): the review labels the Anthology record "[ABS]". The Anthology landing-page abstract is an older wording that does not contain the "source and target" or provenance statements; those are in the PDF (both the Anthology PDF and arXiv v1). The description is correct, but the support is the full text, not the Anthology abstract.
7. FACTWASH (not a priority item): the novelty review says FACTWASH and Manufactured Confidence "investigate memory systems including Mem0 and LangMem"; per the abstracts, LangMem is tested only in Manufactured Confidence.

Checked arithmetic in the red-team review: 1/8 = 12.5%; 600–1,200 items × 8 raters = 4,800–9,600 judgments; mean of the seven scores = 23/7 = 3.29. All correct.
