- Closest works (3–5, more if needed): identifier — what it does — delta — does the delta matter to a referee?

  *Fault-seeding estimator side*
  1. **Sun, "Mutation Testing of Task-Scoped State Oracles in Software-Agent Benchmarks", Research Square rs-10665114 v1, 18 Aug 2026 [abs via publisher page; also described in 2610.09142 full text].** Starts from known-valid executions, injects six classes of harmful state faults plus three benign transformations, reruns the official evaluators of τ²-Bench, ToolSandbox and AppWorld with no model call; 100 of 110 harmful mutations rejected, all ten misses in ToolSandbox (same-record and cross-namespace collateral changes). — Delta: no mobile benchmark; classes not derived from agent failures; reports miss rates only, no translation into false passes of real agents; no validation against audited passes. — Matters: yes for the estimator and the validation; **no** for "agent-free, state-level, class-stratified fault seeding of state checkers", which this already is. *This paper is missing from both idea cards and is closer to T1 steps 2–3 than any work they cite.*
  2. **arXiv 2609.22220 / ICLR 2027 submission Hdwn4xkEHp, "Measuring the Checker: Mutation Analysis for GPU-Kernel Benchmark Oracles" [full].** Kill matrix over 7,384 witnessed mutants, per-family miss rates (8.7% arithmetic … 78.6% precision), witnessed denominator, equivalent-mutant quarantine. Its limitations section restricts the metric to comparative and existence claims and says it cannot certify a passing kernel; the realism probe is 60 problems / 3 bugs. — Delta: different domain; no estimate of real false accepts; no per-model correction. — Matters: the kill-matrix + family-stratification + witnessed-denominator machinery is fully anticipated (under review at the venue family the idea targets); the estimator and its validation are not.
  3. **arXiv 2610.09142 (v3, 16 Nov 2026; also NeurIPS 2026 Verify-Agents uR6EWsGE48) [full].** Hand-written edits of reference runs for AppWorld/WorkArena, confirmed by an independent end-state check; states that its counts give no prevalence estimate, that no model-generated runs are evaluated, and lists a single whole-state frame check per task and a published per-template mutation score as future work. — Delta: as in the cards. — Matters: yes, but note the paper itself names both F1 instruments' targets as open next steps, so the gap is public.
  4. **arXiv 2606.10315 "Catching One in Five" [abs]** and **OpenReview WcR4HK2IoV (COLM 2026) "Below the Reliability Floor" [abs]**, with **arXiv 2601.05420 / ICML 2026 tPcL3fgkjc "Efficient Inference for Noisy LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluation" [idx]**. The first measures an LLM judge's recall of real defects against exhaustive human review and applies the Rogan–Gladen correction to infer a 3–6× undercount (and notes the correction degenerates when the apparent rate is zero — the analogue of T1's "blind modes"); the second uses Rogan–Gladen to recover true agent success from judge-gated loops. — Delta: sensitivity comes from human labels, not from seeded faults; LLM judges, not programmatic state checkers; no failure-mode stratification. — Matters: partly. T1's formula FP = Σ n·s/(1−s) is a stratified Rogan–Gladen / Horvitz–Thompson correction with specificity fixed at 1; a statistics-minded ICML referee will say the estimator is standard and that the only new ingredient is where the sensitivity comes from.
  5. **arXiv 2605.10448 "Can Agent Benchmarks Support Their Scores?" [full].** Confirms the card's numbers (AndroidWorld N=82, native 61.0%, P/F/U 13/28/41, bound [15.9%, 65.9%], 2 benchmark conflicts = target-set false successes; 17 of 41 AndroidWorld case units received "stronger-measurement" conditions, a layer that includes preservation of unrelated state). — Delta: per-record LLM+two-researcher review; wide bounds; no agent-free component. — Matters: yes, but "first look at wrong passes on AndroidWorld" is not available to F1; only "first estimate with interval, per agent, full suite".
  6. **Direct re-grading works that already report agent-specific false passes and rank changes:** arXiv 2603.00520 SWE-ABS [abs] (about a fifth of previously passing patches rejected, top agent drops to fifth); arXiv 2610.10619 TestJack [abs] (about a third of trials judged correct violate requirements); arXiv 2610.16067 [full] (label correction removes 24–34 points per agent and changes the top agent); ICLR 2027 submission 8PIpkkfZ84 "Benchmark Graders Miss What Agents Leave Behind" [abs] (repairing side-effect omissions flips rankings; about a third of flips pick the worse model on held-out outputs); arXiv 2609.34262 [abs] (share of unearned passes per model cohort). — Delta: all obtain the number by strengthening the grader or auditing passes, none predicts it agent-free; none is mobile. — Matters: these weaken claim C3 of T1 and C2 of M2 as *findings* ("oracle error is differential and reorders agents" is established elsewhere); they are also the "why not just fix the checker and re-grade?" objection.

  *Metamorphic inverse-pair side*
  7. **OpenReview 7XacL5fqyu, ACL ARR Aug 2026, "Evaluators That Grade the Encoding: Metamorphic Contracts for State and Rank Evaluation in Agent Benchmarks" [abs].** Metamorphic testing of state evaluators of agent benchmarks: four families of value-preserving rewrites of the recorded state plus two semantic-breaking controls, replayed through six released evaluators (τ-bench, BFCL). — Delta: relations act on the *encoding of one final state* and expose false failures/instability; M2's relations act *across tasks on device state* and expose wrong/extra effects in agent runs. — Matters: yes, the object differs; but "metamorphic testing of agent-benchmark state evaluators" as a phrase is taken. Not cited in either card.
  8. **OpenReview i7FGehiANA, ACL ARR Oct 2026, "The Agent or the World? Verifier Inflation in Persistent-World Agent Evaluation" [abs]**, with **RWjv2Zg0Wa [abs]** (16.8% of 10,000 generated verifiers pass with a no-op agent). End-state verifiers credit goal states that already held; measured with a provenance-guarded twin; 6.9–19.6 points of inflation on pre-satisfiable tasks; nine of 16 audited benchmark settings could credit residue. — Delta: synthetic testbed; provenance check rather than "empty diff"; no mobile. — Matters: relation R4 (pre-satisfied goal) is substantially anticipated in concept; it should be presented as an application of this, not as new.
  9. **arXiv 2610.34167 SABench (NeurIPS 2026 E&D poster xJdBs3VVTg) [abs]**, with **OpenReview eQH5SQYour AgentRelBench [abs]**, **arXiv 2608.14131 LegacyWorld [abs]**, **arXiv 2608.19741 Thinkingbox [abs]**, **arXiv 2611.03575 VeriHarm-Bench [abs]**, **arXiv 2602.11224 Agent-Diff [abs]**, **arXiv 2609.31301 EffectMatch [abs]**. SABench reports that 12.8% of goal-satisfied runs still make unauthorized writes and that collateral-damage rates vary 4.7× across models at comparable goal rates; AgentRelBench computes damage from database state diffs over repeated runs with no LLM in the path. — Delta: each needs a per-task authored oracle or expected diff in a new benchmark; none audits the shipped checker of an existing mobile benchmark; none is label-free in M2's sense. — Matters: the *phenomenon* behind M2's C1/C2 (side effects among passes are common and agent-dependent) is already published for tool-use agents; M2's novelty is the instrument, not the finding.
  10. **OpenReview JsrKw3nUSV, NeurIPS 2026 Verify-Agents, MORPH-DA [abs].** Metamorphic relations (transform the tabular environment, check output relations of a fixed agent program) with no gold answer; finds 20–26% of apparently correct programs are accidental corrects. — Delta: data-analysis programs, relations over input transformations, not cross-task state round-trips. — Matters: it is the nearest instance of "metamorphic relations expose wrong passes without labels", so the general concept is not new; the cross-task/state form is.
  11. **Invariants on the grader rather than the task:** arXiv 2610.16067 [full] (INV-1…INV-5: an oracle call must not change graded state, two calls must agree, verdicts must not depend on scoring order) and GWFSBzaPNd [abs] (evaluator non-interference). — Delta: idempotence/commutation of *oracle calls*, not of *tasks*. — Matters: a referee may see M2's R2/R3 as the task-level sibling of these; they are distinct objects.
  12. Lineage a referee may raise: arXiv 2402.08699 round-trip correctness for code LLMs [abs] (round trip as unsupervised evaluation); Zaeem, Prasad, Khurshid, ICST 2014, app-agnostic oracles for user-interaction features of mobile apps [idx — bibliographic record only, abstract not read].

  *Evidence a referee can use against T1's coupling assumption (not anticipation, but must be cited):* OpenReview fpeLFP6JEE "AUDIT" (JUDGe 2026 oral) [abs] — about four in five injected errors were unusable test items, and injection fails entirely for relation-valued dimensions; arXiv 2609.00038 trajectory-judge [abs] — recall on injected faults can credit a judge with detection it lacks unless paired against clean parents; arXiv 2609.15015 [abs] — planted perturbation keys are not judge operating characteristics; 2609.22220 [full] — adequacy is relative to the fault model.

- Parts of the idea already anticipated (be specific, claim by claim; treat the fault-seeding estimator and the metamorphic inverse-pair instrument separately):

  **Fault-seeding estimator (IDEA_T1)**
  - *Mutating/seeding faults to audit agent-benchmark graders* — anticipated many times in 2026: 2610.09142 [full], Sun rs-10665114 [abs], zh33pqlBjf "Red-Teaming the Grader" [abs], 5ijFZfVQoy "Passing Without Repairing" [abs] (Kubernetes mutants against a state-reading oracle), 2610.16067 [full] (fault injection into the oracle), arXiv 2611.25179 SimAudit [abs] (recreates instruction/checker disagreement scenarios in simulation: 40 phantom successes), 2609.22220 [full].
  - *Agent-free, state-level seeding stratified by fault class, with per-class miss rates (the kill matrix)* — anticipated by Sun (six harmful classes, state oracles, no model call) and by 2609.22220 (kill matrix, family-skewed misses, witnessed denominator, equivalent-mutant quarantine). T1's M5 "collateral change" result is specifically pre-empted in direction: Sun's ten misses and all confirmed false positives in 2610.09142 are unrequested/collateral changes. T1 claim C1 ("survival concentrates in collateral change") would be a replication on mobile.
  - *Known-answer controls (gold run must pass, null agent must fail)* — 2610.16067 CTL-1/CTL-2 [full]; null-agent probes in RWjv2Zg0Wa and 5ijFZfVQoy [abs].
  - *Correction formula* — Rogan–Gladen-type correction of an imperfect evaluator's count is used for agents in 2606.10315 and WcR4HK2IoV [abs] and analysed in 2601.05420 [idx]; the measurement model with differential error and the ranking-inversion condition is in 2609.25173 §V-B [full] (the card's description of that paper is accurate; it also says such error is unmeasured).
  - *Agent-specific false-pass rates and rank changes as an empirical fact* — SWE-ABS, TestJack, 2610.16067, 8PIpkkfZ84, 2609.34262 (coding / tool-use; obtained by re-grading or auditing).
  - *Wrong passes on AndroidWorld exist* — 2605.10448 [full].
  - *Safety arm (C4)* — nothing found on seeded route-variant harms for mobile safety checkers; state-diff-only detection of harm that transcript/judge grading scores as safe is reported in eQH5SQYour [abs] for enterprise tools.

  **Metamorphic inverse-pair instrument (IDEA_M2)**
  - *Metamorphic testing applied to agent-benchmark state evaluators* — 7XacL5fqyu [abs], but with single-state rewrite relations.
  - *Metamorphic relations as a label-free detector of wrong "passes"* — MORPH-DA [abs], other domain and relation type.
  - *R4 pre-satisfied goal* — i7FGehiANA and RWjv2Zg0Wa [abs].
  - *Idempotence / order-independence as invariants* — on oracle calls only (2610.16067, GWFSBzaPNd).
  - *Whole-state diff as a frame check; cost of legitimately varying fields* — proposed (not done) in 2610.09142 future work [full]; "allowed-state-delta regression tests" argued for by Sun [abs]; per-task versions implemented in Agent-Diff, EffectMatch, Thinkingbox [abs].
  - *Side effects among successful runs are prevalent and agent-dependent* — SABench, AgentRelBench, VeriHarm-Bench, LegacyWorld [abs].
  - *Round trip as an unsupervised correctness signal* — 2402.08699 [abs] (code LLMs).

- Parts with no prior work found:
  - **T1-core:** converting seeded-fault detection probabilities into an estimate, with an interval, of the number of false passes of a *specific* agent from that agent's *failed* runs (Mills/Horvitz–Thompson use of mutation survival). No arXiv or OpenReview record found that does this for any agent benchmark; the mutation-audit papers explicitly stop at survival counts (2610.09142, 2609.22220, Sun).
  - **T1-validation:** a preregistered test of whether seeded-fault detectability matches real agent-failure detectability for benchmark graders (prediction vs blinded audit of passes). The only evidence found on this coupling concerns LLM judges and is negative or cautionary (AUDIT, trajectory-judge).
  - **T1-operators:** fault operators derived from observed agent failure modes and produced through the app UI on an Android emulator; kill matrices for AndroidWorld / AndroidLab / MobileWorld checkers; any mutation or seeding study of a mobile safety benchmark's checker.
  - **M2-core:** cross-task metamorphic state relations (task ; inverse returns to start, idempotence of ensure-goals, commutation of disjoint tasks) used on *agent-generated* runs to certify wrong or extra effects among checker-passed runs, with a noise mask measured from repeated gold round-trips. No record found on arXiv or in the OpenReview sample, for mobile or any other stateful agent benchmark.
  - **M2-C3:** a test of whether gold-run mutation scores predict violation prevalence on agent runs.
  - **Merged:** two independent label-free instruments for the same quantity, cross-validated against a human audit, on a mobile benchmark.
  - Caveat: 0 hits for arXiv abs:"false positive" AND AndroidWorld, and for OSWorld + "false positive(s)", reflects abstract wording, not absence of discussion inside papers.

- Verdict: **incremental** — no record anticipates either core (seeded-fault → per-agent false-pass estimate; cross-task state relations on agent runs), so the idea is not anticipated; but taken claim by claim, most of what surrounds the cores is published or under review. For the estimator: agent-free class-stratified state mutation of agent-benchmark oracles (Sun; 2609.22220 incl. kill matrix) and the Rogan–Gladen-style correction for agent evaluation (2606.10315, WcR4HK2IoV, 2601.05420, 2609.25173) both exist, so the contribution reduces to (a) taking sensitivity from seeded faults per failure mode, (b) validating that against audited passes, (c) the Android setting; a referee can call (a) a recombination, which makes (b) the paper. For the metamorphic instrument: narrower but cleaner — **novel in its specific form** on the evidence found, with R4 anticipated and the motivating finding (side effects among passes, differential across agents) already published for tool-use agents. Sub-verdicts: fault-seeding estimator = incremental; metamorphic inverse-pair instrument = novel (narrow); merged F1 = incremental-to-novel, defensible only if the preregistered validation and the M2 prevalence bound are the headline. Scoop risk is high: 2610.09142 lists both directions as future work, and at least eight grader-audit papers are under review at ICLR 2027.
