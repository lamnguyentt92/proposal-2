## - Closest works (3–5, more if needed): identifier — what it does — delta — does the delta matter to a referee?

**None of the first four entries' key content is reflected in IDEA_L1's related-work list** (AuthMem-Bench and AgentMemGate are listed there, but only at abstract level and without the points below).

1. **FACTWASH, arXiv:2608.03372** (4 Aug 2026; single author, A. Kwon) — *full text*. Names "factwashing": a memory rewrite keeps the claim and drops who said it, how sure they were, and when it held. Ships a write-time gate with seven checks whose first five are POLARITY, CONDITION, HEDGE, ATTRIBUTION, TEMPORAL — i.e. five of IDEA_L1's operator classes — each detected from surface-cue lexicons, with typed change labels (DROPPED attribution, STRENGTHENED certainty, REVERSED polarity). Reports that explicit negation cues are enumerable (0.91 F1) while hedge and attribution lexicons stall near half recall and catch 29% of phrasings outside the lists; 55% of bad writes in constructed conversational hearsay are stance loss vs 7% in business email; unmodified mem0 2.0.7 flagged on 5 of 8 hedged-hearsay writes. Its limitations section names "modality, quantifier scope, evidentiality and reported-speech verbs" as what should be predicted and measured next.
   *Delta:* every check is a single operator read off cue presence; no item pairs with identical cue inventory and opposite gold; no nesting; no measure of licensed facts lost; English; pilot scale (101 labels, one annotator who is the author, κ 0.47).
   *Does it matter to a referee?* Yes, in both directions. It takes away the framing novelty ("a write is a report with its frame deleted") and the single-operator inventory. It also hands IDEA_L1 its strongest motivation: FACTWASH is the cue-based policy that the twins are designed to break. Must be cited and used as a baseline.

2. **Manufactured Confidence, arXiv:2606.29279** (28 Jun 2026; cs.CR; same author) — *full text*; with its follow-up **arXiv:2608.06953** "Explicit, Not Longer" — *abstract*. Shows mem0 and LangMem turn "a user mentioned Alice was probably promoted" into a dated flat fact (5/5 phrasings; 4/5–5/5 across four extractor backbones); Zep keeps the modal hedge (0/12) but drops attribution (12/12) and hearsay (11/12). Tests a hedge-preserving extraction prompt on hearsay *and* on genuinely confident statements (wrong-grant 0.45 → 0.10, confident statements unaffected) and states that this "answers the objection that preserving modality must cost utility". Recommends keeping the as-stated phrasing in the store rather than tagging it.
   *Delta:* reports are about a third party's status in an access-control scenario, not third-party content filed under the user; operators are never nested; tiny n; no linguistically typed gold; English.
   *Does it matter?* Yes. It is direct prior evidence for the half of C1 that says "on single-operator items both block and license can be high at once", and it pre-empts "nobody has looked at reported speech at write time". The follow-up (matched notes that differ only in where the stance sits, 60 claims × 7 registers) is one more matched-pair write-time study.

3. **LAPSE, "Memory Consolidation Flattens the Temporal Shape of User Facts", arXiv:2609.36457** (v2 11 Nov 2026; Univ. of Southern Mississippi, same lab as SEAM; ACL-style manuscript) — *full text*. A linguistically motivated minimal-pair benchmark for memory writers: user statements that differ only in grammatical form (progressive vs simple present, same lexeme), factorial design, pre-registered McNemar tests on matched pairs, separate write-time and read-time tests. 210 of 381 confirmatory pairs flatten one way and none the other; holds in 11 models and in mem0 2.0.19, Graphiti 0.30.1, Letta 0.16.8. A four-field record that keeps the user's words restores the read-time difference that free-text notes erase.
   *Delta:* one operator family (aspect); the gold write decision never flips inside a pair (only the form to be preserved does); no attribution, quotation, negation or conditionals; no nesting; synthetic templates; English only (its limitations say the scoring "may not transfer to … multilingual memories").
   *Does it matter?* Yes. "Controlled minimal pairs, held-constant lexical material, write vs read separated, framework-native writers" is no longer a new benchmark genre for memory. A referee will read IDEA_L1 as the next member of a series (aspect → hedge → attribution → scope composition). The same lab has now published on source boundaries (SEAM) and on write-time form loss (LAPSE); an attribution/quotation follow-up from them is a concrete scoop risk.

4. **AuthMem-Bench, arXiv:2608.01679** (v2 4 Aug 2026; Tsinghua / ECNU) — *full text* (IDEA_L1 had it at abstract level). Paired histories H⁺/H⁻ hold the focal claim and the later task fixed and vary only whether the source licenses the use: 50 base histories × 7 transition templates = 350 pairs. One template is exactly "third-party report p → user-profile fact p" (R2F); others include "external suggestion → user preference" and "assistant suggestion → user decision". Write-time metrics separate wrongful promotion from loss: Upgrade-all, Ret⁻ (focal claim retained on the non-authorising side) and Ret⁺ (retained on the authorised side), reported so that "avoiding upgrades through omission" is visible. Seven consolidators × seven backbones (collapse in 48 of 49), a source-aware consolidator prompt, and predicted authority metadata persisted to read time (unauthorised actions 16.9% → 0.0%).
   *Delta:* the non-authorising source is a different *channel* (tool output, external e-mail, assistant turn), not a report embedded in the user's own utterance; the pair differs in carrier, so cue inventory is not matched; no operator nesting; built on τ²-Bench / APIGen-MT; LLM-judged; English.
   *Does it matter?* Yes. The license-twin / block-twin logic and the paired "promotion vs retention" read-out already exist at the channel level. IDEA_L1 must present its twins as moving that design *inside the utterance* and its metrics as adaptations of Upgrade-all / Ret⁻ / Ret⁺ as well as of AgentMemGate's.

5. **AgentMemGate, arXiv:2610.07707** — *full text* (pp. 1–2, 4–9, 11–12). Beyond what IDEA_L1 records: §6.2 states that the ungated cautious extractor's "protection tracks wording, not evidence" — zero contamination at the two hedged levels, rising as plans sound more settled — and the discussion says "Prompting is unstable". It also prices caution: of 80 hedged plans the cautious style fails to extract the new state for 22 and loses 18 more at field matching; cautious-ungated contamination on the harder held-out set is 10.2%, not 2.5%; over-holding control 1 of 15. A read-time alternative (commit everything typed as a plan, mask at read) matches the gate on contamination but leaves 85–90% of plan entries unresolved.
   *Delta:* one operator family, graded hedge strength rather than matched-cue twins; cost is measured as lost coverage of plans, not as lost *licensed* user facts on a paired item; no nesting, quotation or attribution; LLM-generated English.
   *Does it matter?* Yes. IDEA_L1's problem paragraph says nobody has measured whether the cautious fix is capability or cue-triggered; AgentMemGate already says in print that it tracks wording and already shows a cost. The sentence has to be narrowed.

6. **CASK, "Whose Memory Is It? Scope-Aware Commit Rules for Long-Term LLM Memory"**, OpenReview `jXE0A1Pv0T`, NeurIPS 2026 workshop LP4FM poster — *abstract*. ## - Parts of the idea already anticipated (be specific, claim by claim):

- **Framing — "a memory write is a speech report whose frame has been deleted".** Anticipated in substance by FACTWASH ("keeps a claim while washing away … who said it, how sure they were"), Manufactured Confidence, and AuthMem-Bench ("preserves a claim while erasing the source constraints"); CASK states the licensing view ("the world, branch, or speaker that licenses a proposition"). What is not anticipated is the explicit licensing *condition* (every operator on the path must be commitment-transparent for the user).
- **Problem paragraph — "what nobody has measured is whether that fix is a real semantic capability or a cue-triggered refusal to write".** Partly anticipated. AgentMemGate §6.2: the cautious extractor's protection "tracks wording, not evidence". FACTWASH: a cue lexicon reaches 29% on phrasings outside its lists. Manufactured Confidence: the same evidential marker is obeyed on one model and discounted on another. The cost side is also partly measured (AgentMemGate: 40 of 80 hedges lost before the gate; AuthMem-Bench: Ret⁺ vs Ret⁻).
- **"None [of the existing benchmarks] contains items whose cue inventory is held constant while the correct write decision flips."** Survives only in a narrow reading. AuthMem-Bench holds the claim constant while the correct decision flips (but changes the carrier). LAPSE holds lexical material constant (but the decision does not flip). The sentence must say "inside a single user utterance, with matched cue words".
- **Approach step 1, the frame tuple.** Polarity, condition, hedge, attribution and temporal limit are FACTWASH's five stance/scope checks; evidence span + authority witness is AuthMem-Bench's annotation; "semantic obligations" is DerivAudit's (per the audit). The six-way commitment class and the rule table that derives the write decision were not found.
- **Layer A single-operator variants** (report-other, hedge, conditional, negation, temporal limit): anticipated across Manufactured Confidence, FACTWASH, AgentMemGate, AuthMem-Bench (R2F, R2P, S2D) and MemAlign's attribution_confusion.
- **Paired design and paired accuracy.** Partly anticipated: AuthMem-Bench (350 H⁺/H⁻ pairs), LAPSE (matched pairs, McNemar), the ARR "twin-history probe" (joint accuracy), arXiv:2608.06953 (matched notes).
- **C1, single-operator half** ("on single-operator items both can be high at once"): Manufactured Confidence's hedge-preserving prompt is a small-n instance. The twin-dissociation half was not found.
- **C4 and step 5, lazy disquotation.** The design is anticipated several times (item 10 above). AgentMemGate's plan-typed baseline already produced an "errors relocate" finding: same contamination, 85–90% of entries left unresolved. Only the speech-report-specific accounting on depth ≥ 2 items is open.
- **Metrics** (false unconditional promotion, licensed-fact retention): adaptations of AuthMem-Bench's Upgrade-all / Ret⁻ / Ret⁺ in addition to the two sources IDEA_L1 already credits.
- **Non-English memory evaluation as such:** anticipated (EN/ES/HI/ZH parallel benchmark at PALM; BabelState; plus RUMBA and TimlMemBench from the audit).
- **Null subjects after a reporting verb are hard for LLMs:** prior evidence exists for Chinese (arXiv:2605.31056; `2026.emnlp-main.2056`), not for memory writing.

## - Parts with no prior work found:

- Twins **inside one user utterance** that share the same cue words and have opposite gold write decisions (factive vs non-factive under negation; quote-self vs quote-other; conditional-over-report vs report-over-conditional), scored as paired accuracy for memory writers.
- **Factive / non-factive / counter-factive attitude verbs** as a variable at memory-write time, in any language.
- **Depth ≥ 2 operator nesting** for memory writing, and a **seen vs held-out operator-combination split** (C2). No hit in arXiv, ICLR 2027, ARR 2026, NeurIPS 2026 or the 2026 Anthology volumes screened.
- **Vietnamese Layer B in full:** frame-flip pairs (string-identical target turn, holder changes with the xưng–hô dyad), null subject under *bảo*, the *biết / nghĩ / tưởng* triple, negation without *không*. No Vietnamese memory benchmark, and no LLM-era Vietnamese evaluation of kin-term person reference in dialogue, was found on arXiv, OpenReview (ICLR 2027: 8 "Vietnamese" hits, ARR 2026: about a dozen, none on dialogue, pronouns or memory) or the 2026 Anthology volumes (one hit: a code-mixed MT corpus, `2026.eacl-long.342`).
- **C3:** same-holder rate on pairs, and the claim that translate-to-English-then-extract fails because translation forces an early person choice.
- Human-authored, double-annotated gold with an "ambiguous" class and admissible-set scoring for write decisions (the neighbours use LLM generation plus LLM judges or one annotator).

## - Verdict: novel | incremental | anticipated — reason

**incremental** — no single paper anticipates the package, but the English core is now one step beyond a dense cluster of June–November 2026 preprints, most of which IDEA_L1 does not cite. In print already: the frame-deletion view of memory writing and hearsay/attribution/hedge/condition/polarity loss at write time (FACTWASH, Manufactured Confidence); linguistically controlled minimal pairs for memory writers with framework-native systems (LAPSE); paired license/block histories with separate promotion and retention metrics (AuthMem-Bench); the observation that the cautious extractor tracks wording and has a coverage cost (AgentMemGate); and keep-the-frame record designs (five precedents). What remains unanticipated, as far as these searches reach, is (a) cue-matched opposite-gold twins within the user's utterance with nested operators and a held-out-combination split, and (b) the Vietnamese dyad-anchored perspective items. Those two parts would read as **novel** on their own; a referee who knows the cluster will judge the rest as refinement. The submission survives novelty review only if it leads with (a) and (b), cites and baselines FACTWASH, LAPSE, AuthMem-Bench and Manufactured Confidence, rewrites the "nobody has measured" sentence, and demotes lazy disquotation to a replication-style probe. Scoop risk is higher than the idea card states: two single-lab series (Kwon; AIMS Lab at USM) are each one paper away from attribution/scope twins, and the FACTWASH limitations section names reported-speech verbs and scope as next targets.

**Not searched / not reachable (limits of this verdict):** Google Scholar; Semantic Scholar full-text search (citations endpoint only, rate-limited); OpenReview reviews and PDFs (403 challenge on the forum endpoint) and non-public ARR submissions; anything past the top 200 hits of each OpenReview query; arXiv listings by day (the export API showed low phrase recall); LREC 2026, COLING 2026, AACL 2026, *SEM 2026, CRAC 2026, NAACL 2026 and all 2026 workshop volumes other than CODI and SIGDIAL; Vietnamese-language venues (VLSP and local journals); Hugging Face datasets and GitHub (Mem0 / Zep / Letta issue trackers and prompt repositories); pre-2025 literature except where it surfaced by accident (dialogue-summarisation perspective shift, persona/attribute extraction, MegaVeridicality / CommitmentBank / FactBank lines). Full texts not opened: CASK, the ARR "Anticipation Trap" paper, "Same Conversation, Different Languages", DISCERN, BabelState, "The Subject Is the Signal", arXiv:2608.06953. DerivAudit, MemAlign, TimlMemBench, PASB and Gated Memory were not re-read; statements about them rest on the earlier audit.
