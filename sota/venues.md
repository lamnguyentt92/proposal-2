## 2. Page limits, format, supplementary, anonymity, rebuttal, reproducibility, per-author limits, dual-submission and preprint policy

### Takeaway
IJCAI is the tightest format (7 pages body + 2 pages references, USD 100 fee unless every author has only one submission, max 8 submissions per author, in-person presentation mandatory); ICML and ACL long papers both allow 8 content pages with unlimited references/appendix. All three are double-blind, allow arXiv preprints, forbid dual submission, and now require a qualified reciprocal reviewer/service contributor per paper.

### Cited Findings

**IJCAI 2027**
- Page limit: "no longer than 9 pages in total: 7 pages for the body of the paper (including all figures/tables), plus up to 2 additional pages with references"; optional ethics statement may sit in body or reference pages; overlength or style-violating papers rejected without review; LaTeX/Word kit at ijcai.org/authors_kit; PDF. — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/)
- Supplementary: up to 50 MB, PDF or ZIP (appendices, proofs, data, code), anonymized; no acknowledgements or contribution statements in submissions. Reviewers are not required to read supplementary material. — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/); [IJCAI 2027 Reproducibility](https://2027.ijcai.org/reproducibility/)
- Double-blind; third-person self-citation; anonymize code, URLs, PDF metadata; violations rejected without review. — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/)
- Two-phase review: Phase 1 = two PC reviews; "two good-quality reviews that are not sufficiently positive" means summary rejection with no author response. Survivors get full review, an author response ("concise ... not intended to create a dialogue"), and a meta-review. Criteria: novelty/originality, correctness, clarity, significance, potential impact, presentation quality. — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/)
- Submission limit: "no more than 8 submissions across the Main Track and all Special Tracks combined" per author. Up to three content-area keywords, list available on the submission site. Copyright retained by IJCAI, open access. — [IJCAI 2027 Submission Process](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-submission-process/)
- Primary Paper Initiative: USD 100 per submission, waived when none of the authors appears on any other IJCAI 2027 submission; applies to main and special tracks. — [Primary Paper Initiative](https://2027.ijcai.org/primary-paper-initiative/)
- Reciprocal reviewing: each submission designates one qualified author (or requests exemption) by the full-paper deadline; each author can be designated for at most two submissions; qualified = at least five full papers across IJCAI, AAAI, ECAI, AAMAS, KR, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, EMNLP, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, UAI, AISTATS, COLT, ICAPS, KDD, AIJ, or serving as PC/SPC/AC; exemption only if no author qualifies; reviewer failure in Phase 1 or 2 leads to desk rejection of the papers that designated them. — [Reciprocal Review Policy](https://2027.ijcai.org/reciprocal-review-policy/); [Submission Process](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-submission-process/)
- Reproducibility: authors "must follow the reproducibility guidelines ... and checklist at the time of paper submission"; reviewers score CONVINCING / CREDIBLE / IRREPRODUCIBLE; the guideline page says "We do not expect authors to submit this form"; lack of code/data/full proofs cannot be the key reason for rejection; hyper-parameter ranges and final values, and pseudocode for new algorithms, are expected. — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/); [Reproducibility](https://2027.ijcai.org/reproducibility/)
- Dual submission: not accepted if, at full-paper submission, the paper is under review, published or accepted at a journal or venue with formal proceedings (DOI/ISBN/ISSN as guideline); may not be submitted elsewhere during review; workshops without formal proceedings exempt. Preprints: non-anonymous arXiv/social-media preprints "will not result in rejection". — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/)
- Attendance: at least one author must present in person at Kyoto or Hengqin, both orally and as a poster, or the paper is excluded from the proceedings (exceptions need prior approval). — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/)

**ICML 2027**
- "8 pages for the main paper, with unlimited pages for references, the impact statement, and appendices", single file; camera-ready gets one extra page (9); over-length main body is automatically rejected. LaTeX only, ICML 2027 style, US letter; submission PDF max 50 MB. — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers); [Author Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorInstructions) (both via page summary)
- Optional supplementary files limited to supplementary manuscripts and code/data, anonymized, no separate deadline; no camera-ready supplementary material. Code submission encouraged; "Reproducibility of results and easy availability of code will be taken into account". No checklist is mentioned on the author-instructions page. — [Author Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorInstructions) (via page summary)
- Double-blind; arXiv preprints and talks allowed, but the work must never be advertised as an ICML submission during review. — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers) (via page summary)
- New two-stage reviewing: area chairs decide whether a paper proceeds to full review; first-stage rejects confirmed by a senior AC, with a short justification; "when in doubt, papers go to full review". — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers) (via page summary)
- Author response exists after initial reviews; no revised PDF may be uploaded during the feedback period. — [Author Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorInstructions) (via page summary)
- Mandatory impact statement (separate section before references, outside the page limit; a standard one-line statement is permitted). Mandatory AI Disclosure Statement (outside page limit). Lay summary required at camera-ready. — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers) (via page summary)
- Quotas: "No author may appear on more than 20 submissions"; one reciprocal reviewer per submission, each designable for at most 6 submissions; authors with 3+ submissions must serve on the program committee; an author not eligible as reviewer may appear on at most 2 submissions; each author may be on at most one submission with no eligible reviewer; excess submissions are desk rejected at random at the full-paper deadline. — [Reciprocal Reviewing and Submission Quotas](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/ReciprocalReviewingAndSubmissionQuotas) (via page summary)
- Authors with multiple submissions must provide a self-ranking of their papers. Author additions/deletions after submission are not allowed. — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers); [Author Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorInstructions) (via page summary)
- Dual submission: identical or substantially similar papers may not be submitted elsewhere; concurrent ICML submissions with overlapping authors are treated as prior work; non-archival workshops are fine. — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers) (via page summary)
- Attendance "is optional"; authors choose in-person presentation or proceedings-only (virtual registration needed). — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers) (via page summary)

**ACL 2027 (via ARR)**
- Long papers: up to 8 pages of content; short papers: up to 4 pages; unlimited references; required "Limitations" section and optional ethics section after the conclusion do not count; missing Limitations or over-length means desk rejection. Accepted papers get one extra page (9 / 5). — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp); [ACL 2027 Call for Papers](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/)
- Official ACL style files, unmodified; anonymized supplementary materials; links to download-tracking hosts such as Dropbox not allowed. — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- Two-way anonymized review. "Beginning February 15, 2024, there is no anonymity period or limitation on posting or discussing non-anonymous preprints"; anonymous submissions are incentivized via special awards and priority for borderline papers; choosing the binding "no non-anonymous preprint" option commits authors not to preprint until meta-reviews are released. — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- Two-step process: ARR reviews + meta-review, then commitment to ACL where Senior Area Chairs and Program Chairs decide; author list cannot change at commitment; papers not selected for the main conference are automatically considered for Findings. — [ACL 2027 Call for Papers](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/)
- Responsible NLP Research checklist is mandatory; incorrect or misleading answers can cause desk rejection. — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- Sustainable reviewing policy (from October 2026): review is guaranteed only if the submission brings a qualified service contributor who vouches for it (max 2 submissions per contributor); others enter a lottery and may be desk rejected; caps of 20 submissions per author and 5 as first/joint-first author per cycle. — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp); [Sustainable reviewing policy](https://aclrollingreview.org/sustainable-reviewing-2026)
- Background given by ARR: about 17K submissions in May 2026, 38% from authors with no available publication record. — [Sustainable reviewing policy](https://aclrollingreview.org/sustainable-reviewing-2026)
- Multiple submission: ARR "precludes multiple submissions" with any journal or archival conference/workshop (IJCAI, ICML, AAAI, NeurIPS named); commitment counts as "under review". Text reuse capped at 10% of tokens; concurrent related submissions by overlapping authors must cite each other. — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- Resubmission: must link the previous version; meta-review score 1.5 cannot be resubmitted in the next cycle, score 1 never; withdrawal later than 48 h after the deadline blocks resubmission until the second subsequent cycle. Papers within 3 months of the deadline count as contemporaneous. — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- Presentation required (in-person or virtual options) for inclusion in the proceedings. — [ACL 2027 Call for Papers](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/)

### Inferences
- For a first-time author team with no one meeting the reviewer bar: IJCAI allows an exemption request, ICML allows at most one such paper per author, and ARR sends the paper to a capacity lottery (risk of desk rejection without review). ARR is therefore the riskiest of the three for a team without a qualified contributor.
- IJCAI's 7-page body is one page shorter than ICML/ACL; benchmark-style work with large tables depends on the (optional-to-read) supplement there.

## 3. Policy on use of LLMs / generative AI by authors

### Takeaway
IJCAI is the strictest (polishing only; no AI-generated content), ICML is the most permissive (any use allowed if substantive use is disclosed in a mandatory AI Disclosure Statement), and ACL/ARR sits between (graded cases a-f, disclosure in the Responsible NLP checklist and Acknowledgements). All three bar AI authorship and treat hidden prompt injections in the PDF as grounds for rejection.

### Cited Findings

**IJCAI 2027**
- Key sentence: "LLMs must not be used to generate new content." Authors may use LLMs only to polish text they wrote; authors carry full responsibility for plagiarism and correctness; LLM-generated text that is part of the methodology or experimental analysis is allowed if clearly labelled and described. — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/)
- IJCAI Generative AI Policy (last updated 11 Nov 2026; CFPs may add rules but not relax it): AI tools cannot be authors or be cited as a source; no generation of "text, ideas, arguments, analyses or references"; AI must not create or alter images representing primary observed/experimental data; "Hidden text or instructions aimed at automated reviewing tools are forbidden". Reviewers may not upload submissions to generative AI tools. — [IJCAI Generative AI Policy](https://ijcai.org/generative_ai_policy/)

**ICML 2027**
- Key phrase: use is permitted "provided that their use is appropriately disclosed". Every submission needs an AI Disclosure Statement at the end of the paper (outside page limit) covering substantive use: generating ideas, deriving/assisting proofs, writing substantial code, analysing or generating data, interpreting results, producing figures, drafting substantial text. Routine editing (spelling, grammar, formatting, LaTeX, autocomplete) needs no disclosure. AI used as part of the method (annotation, evaluation, synthetic data) must be described in the main paper. AI cannot be an author; authors answer for fabricated citations and errors; non-disclosure or misrepresentation can lead to desk rejection; AI use by itself should not count for or against a paper. — [ICML 2027 Generative AI policy for authors](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/UseOfGenerativeAIByAuthors) (via page summary)
- Prompt injection is listed among prohibited conduct in the CFP. Reviewers follow a single policy (ICML 2026 "Policy B"): privacy-compliant LLMs may help understand papers and polish reviews, but "Delegating judgement and critique of the paper to LLMs is not allowed." — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers) (via page summary)

**ACL 2027 / ARR**
- Key sentence: "generative AI tools do not qualify for authorship." Use for writing or coding, and its scope, "must be disclosed in the Responsible NLP Checklist", with details in Acknowledgements (and README for code). — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- Graded cases: (a) language polishing and (b) predictive typing need no disclosure; (c) literature search is fine with normal citation diligence; (d) low-novelty generated text must be flagged and verified; (e) AI-suggested new ideas must be disclosed; (f) a generative model cannot be a co-author. Authors remain fully responsible for correctness and plagiarism checks of text and code. — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- "Submissions with prompt injections intended to manipulate generative tools will be desk rejected"; camera-ready papers with such manipulation may be retracted. — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- The ACL 2027 call's "Writing Assistance" item links to the ACL 2023 policy post. — [ACL 2027 Call for Papers](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/)
- Enforcement precedent: ACL 2026 found over 100 accepted camera-ready papers containing citations to non-existent works during final checks. — [ACL 2026 proceedings front matter](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.0.pdf)
- Enforcement precedent: ICML 2026 desk-rejected 497 papers (~2% of submissions) because their reciprocal reviewers violated the LLM reviewing rules. — [ICML blog, 18 Mar 2026](https://blog.icml.cc/2026/03/18/on-violations-of-llm-review-policies/)

### Inferences
- A manuscript whose prose was drafted by an LLM is non-compliant at IJCAI, compliant-with-disclosure at ICML, and must be disclosed (case d/e) at ACL. A single workflow that satisfies all three: human-written text, LLM used for polishing only, any LLM role in the method (judges, synthetic data, simulators) described in the paper.
- Research on prompt injection must present attack strings visibly as research content; hidden text aimed at reviewing tools is the prohibited case under all three policies.

## 4. Topics, tracks and contribution types

### Takeaway
ACL 2027 has an explicit "LLM Agents for NLP" area and a special theme "Homogenization and knowledge collapse in LLMs"; ICML 2027 introduces six contribution types including "Datasets, Evaluations and Benchmarks" and a separate position-paper track; IJCAI 2027 has an any-AI main track plus four special tracks (AI4Science, AI and Health, AI the Arts and Creativity, AI for Social Good) on the same deadlines.

### Cited Findings

**IJCAI 2027**
- Main track: "significant, original, and previously unpublished results on any aspect of AI"; novel problems, novel application domains and cross-discipline papers especially encouraged; a selection of distinguished papers is invited for expedited review at AIJ or JAIR. — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/)
- Four special tracks with the same deadlines: AI4Science; AI and Health; AI, the Arts and Creativity; AI for Social Good. Separate calls with different deadlines: Journal Track, Sister Conferences Track, Senior Researchers' Position Talks, Early Career Highlights, Competitions, Demos, Doctoral Consortium. — [IJCAI 2027 CFP](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-main-track/)
- AI for Social Good 2027 page: full paper 11 Jan 2027, notification 21 Apr 2027, camera-ready 28 May 2027; "The full call for papers ... will be published here soon." — [IJCAI 2027 AI for Social Good](https://2027.ijcai.org/ijcai-2027-call-for-papers-ai-for-social-good/)
- For comparison, the 2026 proceedings sections were: Main Track, AI4Tech, AI and Health, AI and Social Good, AI and Robotics, Human-Centred AI, Survey Track, Early Career Spotlight, Sister Conferences Best Papers, Journal Track, Doctoral Consortium, Demo Track. — [IJCAI 2026 proceedings](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2026/)

**ICML 2027**
- Topics (non-exhaustive): general ML, deep learning, evaluation, theory of ML, ML systems, optimization, probabilistic methods, reinforcement learning, trustworthy ML, application-driven ML. — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers) (via page summary)
- Contribution types (author picks one; guides reviewing and matching): General; Theory ("Empirical validation is not required when the theoretical results stand on their own"); Application-driven ("Merely applying an existing machine learning method to a new dataset would generally not be sufficient"); Concept and Feasibility ("not intended for preliminary or incomplete work"); Negative Results ("A failed experiment alone is not sufficient"); Datasets, Evaluations and Benchmarks (no new learning algorithm required; should "not be evaluated primarily according to whether it enables a new state of the art result"; must address limitations, licensing, privacy, consent). — [ICML 2027 Contribution Types](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/ContributionTypes) (via page summary)
- Position papers: separate track and OpenReview site, call "coming soon"; no impact statement required there. — [ICML 2027 CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers); [Author Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorInstructions) (via page summary)

**ACL 2027**
- Areas listed on the ACL 2027 call (29 incl. theme): Special Theme; Clinical and Biomedical Applications; Computational Social Science and Cultural Analytics; Dialogue and Interactive Systems; Discourse and Pragmatics; Efficient Methods for NLP; Ethics, Bias, and Fairness; Human-Centered NLP and Human-AI Interaction; Information Extraction; Information Retrieval and Text Mining; Interpretability and Analysis of Models for NLP; Language Diversity and NLP for Low-resourced Languages; Language Modeling; LLM Agents for NLP; Linguistic Theories, Cognitive Modeling, and Psycholinguistics; Machine Learning for NLP; Machine Translation; Multilingualism and Cross-Lingual NLP; Multimodality and Language Grounding to Vision, Robotics and Beyond; NLP and Code Models; NLP and Symbolic Reasoning; NLP Applications; Phonology, Morphology, and Word Segmentation; Question Answering; Semantics: Lexical and Sentence-Level; Sentiment Analysis, Stylistic Analysis, and Argument Mining; Speech Recognition, Text-to-Speech and Spoken Language Understanding; Summarization; Syntax: Tagging, Chunking and Parsing. — [ACL 2027 Call for Papers](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/)
- The ARR CFP's area list additionally contains "Generation" and "Resources and Evaluation" (not on the ACL 2027 list as fetched). — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- ARR contribution types: low-resource approaches; low-compute/efficiency; data resources; data analysis; model analysis & interpretability; NLP engineering experiment; publicly available software/pre-trained models; position papers; reproduction study; surveys; theory. Negative results are welcome. — [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)
- Special theme "Homogenization and knowledge collapse in LLMs": empirical, theoretical, survey and position papers on output-diversity collapse, "generative monoculture", metrics for homogenization, mechanisms (training data, alignment, decoding), human-vs-model diversity, cross-linguistic homogenization, polarization; long or short; special session and a Thematic Paper Award planned. — [ACL 2027 Call for Papers](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/)
- Paper kinds invited: negative findings, surveys, new resources, position papers, linguistic insights, reproductions. A short paper "is not a shortened long paper". — [ACL 2027 Call for Papers](https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/); [ARR CFP](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp)

### Inferences
(Drawn from the titles above and the 17 abstracts read; full papers were not read, so depth-of-evaluation details beyond the abstract are not confirmed.)
- Novelty type. Three recurring shapes: (i) a new mechanism (memory architecture, defence, attack) with a named acronym; (ii) a benchmark/dataset plus an analysis of why existing systems fail; (iii) benchmark + method in one paper (AMA-Bench + AMA-Agent, MT-AgentRisk + ToolShield, EAsafetyBench + Pinpoint). Shape (iii) appears at all three venues and looks like the safest pattern for benchmark work.
- Theory depth. Mostly empirical. Formal results appear at ICML ("Provable Defense", a decision-theoretic impossibility proof in the SecFid paper); ACL and IJCAI abstracts in this cluster contain no theorem claims. IJCAI's reproducibility rubric nevertheless rewards formally stated claims and complete proofs when theory is present.
- Evaluation scale. Defence/attack papers evaluate on established public agent benchmarks (AgentDojo in both MELON and Task Shield; Mind2Web and WebArena for AWM) across several open and closed models (4-7 models or frameworks is the range seen in abstracts) and report headline deltas (e.g. ASR 2.07% at 69.79% utility; +11.16% accuracy; F1 +5.2 pp at 12x speed).
- Baselines. Explicit comparison to "SOTA" defences or memory systems is universal; benchmark papers evaluate multiple existing memory frameworks (seven in Mem2ActBench) rather than one.
- Dataset size. Accepted benchmarks in the abstracts read are modest in item count but structured: 500 tasks (SafeArena, 250 safe / 250 harmful), 400 tasks from 2,029 sessions (Mem2ActBench). Size is justified by realism, taxonomy coverage and validated difficulty rather than raw volume.
- Human annotation. Benchmarks cite expert-curated QA (AMA-Bench) or human validation of an automated pipeline (Mem2ActBench: 91.3% judged strongly memory-dependent). Fully synthetic data without a human check was not seen among the benchmark abstracts read.
- Venue fit. ACL is the densest home for conversational/long-term dialogue memory and its benchmarks (LoCoMo-Plus, CloneMem, HyperMem, APEX-MEM) and has a dedicated agents area; ICML takes agent security and memory when framed as a general ML method, a rigorous benchmark, or with a formal result; IJCAI accepts the topics but in small numbers, typically compact method papers fitting 7 pages.
- Crowding. With 45+ ACL 2026 and 100+ ICML 2026 titles in this cluster, an incremental "new memory architecture evaluated on LoCoMo" or "new prompt-injection defence on AgentDojo" faces heavy direct competition; papers that expose a failure of existing evaluations (LoCoMo-Plus, SecFid, PIArena, "Does Memory Need Graphs?") show a differentiating angle.
